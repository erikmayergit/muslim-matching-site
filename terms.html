4. How Muslim Matching works

  • Cards: your card shows your answers and an avatar that you build. It never shows photos.
  • Browse: you see the cards of members of the opposite gender. You choose who can see your card (You → Settings → Who can see my profile), and you can take a break (You → Settings → Manage account).
  • Requests: on the free plan, you can send up to 3 match requests in each calendar month. A request that nobody answers within 30 days expires. When you receive a request on the free plan, you see only the sender's first name, age and country. To see the sender's full card and accept the request, you need Premium.
  • Matches and vetting: when a request is accepted, you each write Profile Feedback on the other's full profile, and then read each other's feedback. Next, you go through five vetting phases: Deal Breakers, Worldview, Marriage Views, Lifestyle and Next Steps.
  • Deadlines: each move has 24 hours. Answering a concern has 48 hours. If neither of you writes Profile Feedback within 7 days, the match closes. When a deadline passes, the match closes.
  • Ending a match: either of you can end a match. During vetting, you must give a short reason, and the other person sees it. A match that has closed cannot be reopened. With Premium, you can ask for one rematch for each match that ended or expired; the other person needs Premium to accept it.
  • The walī: when you both finish all five phases and both choose to move forward to the walī, the brother receives the sister's walī's name, phone number and relationship. This is the only contact detail that Muslim Matching passes on.
  • No chat: the app has no chat, no calls and no photos.
  • What we do not promise: we do not promise that you will find a match or get married.

7. Safety tools, reports and what we do

  • The filter: before a text that other members can see is saved, our filter checks it. This covers your name, bio, profession, education, profile answers, Profile Feedback, vetting answers, concerns and clarifications, end-of-match reasons and rematch reasons. The filter has two automatic steps. First, fixed rules and a word list check the text. If they find nothing, an AI model (Claude, from Anthropic) checks it. The AI model does not see your name. If the AI check is not available, only the first step runs. The filter blocks contact details, plans to meet, and flirting, sexual or abusive language, and tells you why. It also blocks a text that the AI model declines to judge or cannot answer clearly, and asks you to rephrase it. The filter decides only about what you are saving. It never suspends an account; a person decides that. Our Privacy Policy (section 13) explains what the AI model receives. No filter catches everything, so reports remain our backstop: please report anything that the filter misses.
  • Report: on every screen that shows another member, tap "⋯" → Report. Choose a reason: "Rude, threatening or hateful", "Sexual or flirty", "Asked for or shared contact details", "Fake or misleading profile", "Under 18" or "Something else". You can add details (up to 1,000 characters); for "Something else", details are required. Sending a report also blocks the person. Only our team sees your report, and the person is not told about it.
  • Block: tap "⋯" → Block. You stop seeing each other, and any request or match between you ends. The other person is not told; if you had a match, they see "This match has ended." To undo a block, go to You → Settings → Profiles I've blocked → Unblock. A match that ended stays ended.
  • Contact us: you can also write to us in You → Help & support, or email support@muslim-matching.com.
  • What we do with reports: our team checks new reports every day and reviews every report. We act on every report within 24 hours. If the reported content breaks these Terms, we remove the content, and we remove the user who posted it from Muslim Matching. We can also act without a report.
  • Removing a user: when we remove a user, we suspend their account. Their card disappears, their requests are withdrawn, and their matches end. A person on our team makes this decision; no machine suspends an account. For serious or repeated breaches, we may close the account for good.
  • If you think we made a mistake: a suspended account cannot use Help in the app, so email support@muslim-matching.com. A person will review your case and reply.
  • Emergencies: if someone is in danger, contact the police or emergency services first.
