Muslim Matching Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Summary

  • Muslim Matching is an app for Muslims who are looking for marriage. Boring Businesses, LLC runs it.
  • We collect what you tell us, so that we can build your profile and run requests, matches and vetting. This includes your faith, your background and anything you choose to tell us about your health. We use these sensitive details only with your explicit consent. You can withdraw it at any time.
  • Other members see your card. It has no photos. Only a match sees your fuller profile. Your walī's phone number goes only to the brother, at the end of a completed vetting.
  • There are no ads, no tracking and no analytics. We do not sell or share your personal information.
  • A few companies help us run the app: Supabase (our database, in Ireland), Resend (sign-in emails, and report alerts to our team), RevenueCat (subscriptions), Expo (app updates) and Anthropic (an AI check of your texts before they are saved). Apple and Google handle sign-in and payments.
  • You can delete your account at any time in the app: You → Settings → Manage account → Delete account.
  • To get a copy of your data, or for any other privacy request, email us (section 20).
  • Muslim Matching is only for adults. You must be 18 or older.

3.3 Your private answers (only you and our team)

  • Your date of birth.
  • How long you have wanted to get married.
  • Your answers to the reflection questions at sign-up (for example, "Why aren't you married yet?"), and how committed you are.
  • Your settings: who can see your card, and whether you are taking a break.
  • Sisters only: your walī's name, phone number and relationship to you (section 8).

3.4 Your fuller profile (only your matches see it)

These answers add depth to your profile. Only the other person in an active or completed match can see them. They never show in Browse.

  • Your salah (how often you pray).
  • Your education, work pattern, field of work and income band.
  • Your madhab, mindset, religious practices, the scholars you listen to, and how long you have been practising.
  • "Anything someone should know before they swipe?" (free text, up to 500 characters).
  • Your marriage plans, what you are open to (for example, polygyny), the hadiths you choose, who you are looking for, your hard nos and your mahr range.
  • Your personality, your hobbies and "About" (free text, up to 2,000 characters).

3.5 Requests, matches and vetting

  • Match requests: who you sent a request to, who sent you one, and when. For a rematch request, the reason you write (up to 300 characters).
  • Match records: who the match is between, its stage and deadlines, when it ended, who ended it and why. If you write a reason when you end a match, we store it (up to 280 characters).
  • Profile Feedback: the reflection you write on the other person's full profile.
  • Vetting answers in five phases: Deal Breakers (which includes things about you), Worldview, Marriage Views, Lifestyle and Next Steps. You choose topics and write your stance on each one, for example on the topic "Health" or "Debt". Each stance can be up to 800 characters.
  • Your reactions: which of the other person's answers you opened, and how you rated each one (concern, neutral or resonates). Only you see these.
  • Your decisions: your choice at each step to move forward, and to involve the walī.
  • Concerns and clarifications: up to 280 characters each.
  • Drafts: the app saves your unsent answers while you write. Only you see them.
  • An activity log of actions in a match, such as "answer opened" or "draft saved", with the time. Only our team can see it.

3.6 Browse

  • Filters and sort order that you save (for example, age 22–30, sect, background and countries).
  • Passes: when you swipe left, we hide that profile from you for 30 days.
  • When you were last active: nobody sees it, but it orders the "Recently active" sort.

3.7 Premium and payments

  • Subscription status: your plan, the store, when it ends and whether it renews.
  • Purchase records that RevenueCat sends us from Apple or Google, such as the product, price, currency, store country and transaction numbers.
  • The sisters' oath: if you use "I can't afford Premium", we store which version of the oath text you affirmed, and when.
  • We never see your card or bank details. You pay Apple or Google directly.

3.8 Safety

  • Reports you send: the reason you choose, any details you write (up to 1,000 characters), where in the app you were, and the match, if there is one. We keep reports about you in the same way.
  • Blocks: who blocked whom, and when.
  • Filter blocks: when our filter stops a text, we do not save the text. If the AI check stopped it (section 13), Anthropic keeps its copy for the time in section 10: up to 30 days, or up to 2 years if Anthropic's safety systems flag it. We may keep a short record: where it happened, the type of rule (or that the AI model declined to judge the text, or gave no clear answer), and a coded fingerprint (a "hash") of the text, not the text itself.
  • Filter use count: to stop misuse, we count how often your app uses the filter. We keep one record: the start of the current 10-minute window and the number of checks in it. Above 60 checks in 10 minutes, saving pauses until the window ends.
  • Suspensions: if our team suspends an account, we record when and why.

3.9 Support

  • Help & support tickets: the topic (technical issue, billing issue or question) and your message (up to 2,000 characters).
  • Attachments: an image (PNG or JPEG) or a PDF of up to 5 MB, if you add one. Only our team can open it.
  • Emails that you send us, and our replies.

3.10 What the app sends automatically

  • App updates (Expo): when the app checks for an update, it sends Expo a random installation ID, your phone's platform, version details of the app and your IP address. After a crash, it also sends the last error message (up to 1,024 characters). The installation ID stays the same each time you open the app, and your phone's backups include it.
  • Subscriptions (RevenueCat): after you sign in, the app sends RevenueCat your account ID. RevenueCat's software can also receive basic technical details, such as your phone's platform, the app version and your IP address.
  • Server logs (Supabase): our database provider logs each request with details such as the IP address, the device and browser type, and the time.
  • Sign-in (Apple and Google): when you sign in with Apple or Google, the app exchanges sign-in details with them.

3.11 What stays on your phone

We do not collect the following. It stays on your phone.

  • App lock: if you turn on Passcode (You → Settings → Passcode), your phone checks your Face ID, Touch ID or passcode. The app only learns whether the check passed. Nothing goes to us.
  • Your phone's region: the app uses it only to suggest your country. Only the country you choose leaves your phone.
  • Your answers before you sign in.
  • Your sign-in session, until you sign out.
  • A hint that shows how you last signed in ("you last logged in with …"), with your email address partly hidden. It stays after you sign out and after you delete your account, until you delete the app.

3.12 What we do not collect

  • We do not ask for photos of you. The only images we receive are files that you attach to a support ticket. With Google sign-in, we keep a link to your Google profile picture (section 17).
  • No chat messages, calls or voice. The app has no chat.
  • No GPS or precise location.
  • No contacts from your address book.
  • No advertising ID.
  • No face or fingerprint data. Face ID and Touch ID stay on your phone.
  • No payment card details.

The app does not use sign-in by text message (SMS), push notifications, web checkout or analytics today. We will update this policy before we switch any of these on.

4. Sensitive information and your explicit consent

Some of what you share is "special category data" under the GDPR, and "sensitive personal information" under US state laws. We take extra care with it.

What it is:

  • Religious beliefs and practice: how you follow Islam, your sect, whether you reverted, your religious dress and grooming, your salah, madhab, practices and the scholars you listen to, your hadiths, and the oath, if you take it.
  • Ethnic origin: your background. Your avatar's skin tone can also show it.
  • Health: anything you choose to write about your health, for example under the vetting topic "Health", or in "Anything someone should know before they swipe?".
  • Opinions and beliefs in your vetting answers, for example on worldview topics such as "LGBTQ+ in society" or "Feminism framing".
  • Possibly your sex life or your past: some marriage preferences (for example, "Marrying a virgin" or "Open to polygyny"), and anything you choose to write about past relationships or convictions.

Muslim Matching is an app for Muslims. So the fact that you have an account can itself suggest your religion. We protect all of your account data with this in mind.

Our legal reason is your explicit consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a)). You give it when you choose to add these details to your profile, or to write them in your answers. We use them for two purposes: to show your profile and find matches, and to keep texts safe with our filter, including the AI check (section 13). The sign-in screen tells you this before you continue: "By continuing you accept our Terms of Use & EULA. We use your details, like your faith, to find matches, and an AI service checks texts for safety. See our Privacy Policy."

You choose what you share. Some answers are optional. For your sect, you can choose "Prefer not to say".

Who sees it: section 7 explains this. In short, other members who can see your card see the card details, and only your matches see your deeper answers. Our service providers also handle these details for us (section 7.3), for example the Anthropic AI check (section 13).

How to withdraw your consent: you can withdraw it at any time.

  • Remove or change the details in You → Edit profile. For your sect, you can choose "Prefer not to say".
  • Or delete your account: You → Settings → Manage account → Delete account.
  • You can also email support@muslim-matching.com.

Some answers are needed for your card, for example how you follow Islam. Muslim Matching cannot build your card or match you without them. To withdraw your consent for those, delete your account, and we delete the answers. Withdrawing does not make our earlier use of them unlawful.

Please think before you write. Your match can read what you share, and could copy it. Share health or other sensitive details only if you want your match to know them.

6. What you must give us, and what is optional

  • Needed for an account: a way to sign in (email, Apple or Google), your first name, gender, date of birth and country, and the main sign-up answers, including your faith answers. Without them, we cannot create your card or match you.

7. Who can see your information

7.1 Other members

  • Your card: signed-in members of the opposite gender can see it in Browse, if all of these are true:
    • "Who can see my profile" is set to "Everyone" (the default). If you choose "Only people I request", your card shows only to people you have sent a request to, while the request is waiting;
    • you are not taking a break;
    • neither of you has blocked the other;
    • your account is not suspended.
  • When you send a request: if the other person does not have Premium, they first see only your first name, age and country.
  • Your fuller profile: only the other person in an active or completed match.
  • In a match: the other person reads your Profile Feedback (after you have both written it), your vetting answers (after you have both sent your answers for that phase), and any concern or clarification that you write. If you send a rematch request, the other person reads your reason if they have Premium.
  • After a match ends: the other person keeps a small card of you in their Unmatches list (first name, age, country, avatar and Premium badge), and can still open your card. This continues even if you take a break or change who can see your card. It stops when either of you blocks the other or deletes their account, or when either account is suspended.
  • After a completed vetting: you and your match both keep each other's fuller profile, until one of you blocks the other or deletes their account, or either account is suspended.
  • Your end-of-match reason: if you end a match during vetting, the other person sees the reason you write. If you end it at the Profile stage, the reason is optional, and the other person sees only a standard message, but we still store your reason.
  • Your first name outside the app: a member can use the share button in Browse to send your first name and the words "on Muslim Matching" to someone else.
  • Copies: a member can take screenshots of what they see, or copy it. The app cannot stop this.
  • Your saved filters: they decide where you appear when other members sort Browse by "I'm in all their filters". So a member could guess some of your filters by changing their own answers.

8. Your walī's information

This section is for sisters and for their walīs. A walī is a sister's guardian, for example her father or her brother. A walī does not use the app himself.

  • Who is responsible: Boring Businesses, LLC (section 1).
  • What we hold: the walī's name, phone number and relationship to the sister (Father, Brother, Uncle, Grandfather, Son, Imam or Other).
  • Where it comes from: the sister enters it in You → Settings → Walī. She must add it before her final decision to move forward in a vetting. We do not get it from anywhere else.
  • Why we hold it: in Muslim Matching, the walī comes in at the end. When a sister and a brother both finish all five vetting phases, and both choose to move forward to the walī, the brother receives the walī's details. He can then contact her family in the proper way.
  • Who sees it, and when:
    • Nobody sees it while the sister builds her profile or goes through vetting. It is never on her card.
    • Only the brother of a completed match receives it, and only at the end, after both have chosen to involve the walī.
    • He keeps access only while neither of them has blocked the other, and neither account is suspended.
    • The sister sees that her walī's details were shared.
    • Our team can see it when needed (section 7.2).
  • Our legal reason: legitimate interests: the sister's interest, and ours, in letting her involve her walī, as the service promises (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). For the sister, it is also part of our contract with her (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Where it is stored: in our database in Ireland (sections 7.3 and 9).
  • Automatic decisions: we make none about walīs.
  • How long we keep it: the sister's copy stays until she changes it or deletes her account. The copy given to a brother also stays until she deletes her account. If the brother saved the number outside the app, we cannot delete that copy.
  • Sisters: when you add your walī, you confirm with a tick box that he agreed to share his contact details. Please also show him this section.
  • Walīs: we do not contact walīs ourselves; we ask sisters to tell their walī about this section. You have the same rights as members (section 12). You can ask for a copy of your details, ask us to correct or delete them, object to our use of them, or complain to a data protection authority. Email support@muslim-matching.com. If we delete your details, the sister must add a walī again before she can finish a vetting.

11. Deleting your account

In the app: You → Settings → Manage account → Delete account. Deletion happens at once and cannot be undone. If you signed in with Apple on an iPhone, the app asks you to confirm with Apple first.

What happens:

  1. We hide your card, withdraw your waiting requests, and end your active matches. The other person sees "This match has ended."
  2. We ask Apple to remove the link between your Apple account and our app.
  3. We delete your customer record at RevenueCat.
  4. We delete your account and the information linked to it: your sign-in details, card, private answers, fuller profile, settings and filters, the requests you sent, your own vetting answers, reactions, decisions and drafts, the concerns you raised and your Profile Feedback, your walī's details (including any copy given to a brother), your Premium status and oath, your filter-block records and filter use count, passes, and your support tickets and attachments.

What stays:

  • Match records, without your account ID. This includes any reason you wrote when you ended a match, also at the Profile stage, where the other person did not see it.
  • A clarification that you wrote in reply to the other person's concern. It stays with their concern: for a match that ended, until we delete the match content 90 days after the end; for a completed match, until the other person deletes their account.
  • Requests that you received, without your account ID. (We delete the requests that you sent.)
  • The activity log of your matches, without your account ID.
  • Reports that you sent or that others made about you, without the account IDs.
  • Our purchase records, without your account ID or the purchase details.
  • Backups and logs, until they expire (section 10).
  • Texts that Anthropic checked, until Anthropic deletes them: up to 30 days, or up to 2 years if Anthropic's safety systems flagged them, and the results of its safety checks for up to 7 years (section 10).
  • What Apple and Google keep under their own rules.
  • Anything that another member copied, for example your walī's phone number.

13. Automatic decisions

  • The text filter. When you save a text that other members can see, or a reason for ending a match, our filter checks it automatically, in two steps. First, fixed rules and a word list check it. If they find nothing, an AI model (Claude, from Anthropic) checks it. The AI model gets only the text, without your name or account ID (section 7.3). If either step finds contact details, plans to meet, or flirting, sexual or abusive language, the text is not saved, and the app tells you why. If the AI model declines to judge a text, or gives no clear answer, the text is not saved either, and the app asks you to rephrase it. Such a block does not mean that you broke a rule. Your text stays in the field, so you can change it. If the AI check is not available, only the first step checks the text. The filter decides only about what you are saving at that moment. It does not suspend you or limit your account. No filter catches everything, so member reports remain our backstop.
  • People decide about accounts. We may look at repeated filter blocks, but a person on our team reviews them, reviews reports, and decides on any suspension. No machine suspends an account.
  • Fixed rules. Some things happen automatically under the rules in our Terms of Use. For example, a match closes when a deadline passes, and a request expires after 30 days. These rules are the same for everyone. They are not judgements about you.
  • Browse shows profiles by the filters and the sort order that you choose.
  • We do not make any decision about you that is based only on automatic processing and that has a legal or similarly significant effect on you (GDPR Art. 22).
